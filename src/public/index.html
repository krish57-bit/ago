How it works

  • Dates are resolved in code, in your timezone. “Last Tuesday” becomes an exact date before the model is called.
  • Memory is dated. Ask what you asked on any day, or whether an old answer still holds.
  • Claims are checked. Links and figures must come from this turn’s search results; verdicts can’t outrun their evidence.
